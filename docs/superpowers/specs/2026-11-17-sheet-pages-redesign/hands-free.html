D16 · What keys and pedals do

A Bluetooth page-turner pedal is a keyboard: it sends arrows, PageDown/PageUp or Space. Today keys scroll as the browser does and pause autoscroll; ← → change songs in a songbook. Pick one.

← →songbook: previous / next song

B · A small key map

Space or Enter starts and stops autoscroll; PageDown or ↓ turns a screen less two lines; PageUp or ↑ back; a press while scrolling pauses it.

PageDown ↓next screen; while scrolling, jump on
PageUp ↑previous screen

C · B, but a press nudges

As B, except PageDown while scrolling jumps ahead a screen and keeps scrolling (a stray press moves the song on).

D18 · Keeping the screen on

Today the screen stays on only while autoscroll counts in and runs; reading without it (or turning with a pedal), the phone may sleep. Pick one.

A · Today's

On only during autoscroll.

B · A setting

"Keep the screen on" for every sheet page, while the page shows. Costs battery if forgotten.

C · A timer

On for some minutes (say 10) after your last touch or key while a sheet shows, then the phone's own timeout again.

D · The app keeps it on

The Android app keeps the screen on whenever a sheet page shows (a native flag); a laptop browser as today.
