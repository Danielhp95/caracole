# The logbook wording

The owner's ask (2026-11-10): "I want to change the wording of all of the text on the app. I want to give it a more
cohesive style, and make it less verbose. I want the app to feel like a logbook from la horde du contrevent, where
different scribes have their own books, and syncing feels like sharing your logbook with another horde."

Every text was catalogued with a suggestion; the owner chose 229 of them on the review page (51 in their own words)
and asked for the suggestions elsewhere. Branch feature/wording applies them; this file keeps the voice for new text
and the wording of texts on screens not built yet.

## The voice

- Each caracole is a scribe keeping a book; a sync is two hordes sharing logs ("Share logs", "Shared with: X", "Both
  books changed this song. Keep: Phone · Computer"); a file is a copied book; the Requests tab is the Journal.
- The owner keeps "Library" for the tab and its heading ("Library", "Your Library"), and took "In your logbook" for
  the badges.
- Terse, as the owner writes: no articles or possessives where the meaning holds ("Search songs", "Close list",
  "Open sheet", "Newest first", "No matches"), no ellipsis on a running line ("Searching sites"), buttons one verb
  ("Fetch").
- Site trouble is weather ("UG turned us back", "Waiting out UG until Tue 21:00"); no exclamation marks, no
  apologies; digits; dates dd/mm/yy; 24-hour times.
